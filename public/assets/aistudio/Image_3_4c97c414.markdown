## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sensory artistry of an haute patisserie: patient craft, rich tactile indulgence, and uncompromising European refinement. Guided by the ethos *"Baked slow. Finished beautifully,"* the aesthetic bridges timeless culinary tradition with contemporary editorial poise.

The visual style blends luxury editorial minimalism with bespoke tactile accents:
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Cream-tinted foundations replace sterile whites, cultivating an inviting salon experience reminiscent of parchment, dusted flour, and warm marble counters.
- **Opulent Framing:** Deep jewel-toned emerald anchors structure and narrative depth, while burnished gold serves as an exacting, restrained accent reserved for price distinctions, delicate hairline borders, and heirloom emblems.
- **Editorial Pace:** Expansive macro-whitespace, generous breathing room around photography, and disciplined typography invoke the serenity of an archival fine-dining menu.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate tension between the deep, velvety saturation of aged botanical glasshouses and the soft, whipped levity of patisserie creams.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#0F3D34` — Emerald Deep):** The core grounding anchor. Applied to primary CTAs, high-impact dark hero banners, footer foundations, and display headlines on light backdrops.
- **Secondary (`#134E43` — Emerald):** Expressive depth layer. Utilized for interactive states, card surfaces in high-contrast groupings, and tertiary containers.
- **Tertiary (`#C8A96A` — Gold):** Precision luxury accent. Reserved strictly for artisanal seals, currency values, divider filigrees, focus highlights, and bespoke tags. Never deployed for large text blocks or dominant backgrounds.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F7F6F1` — Cream):** The global default canvas. Provides natural warmth, eliminating blue-light glare and framing product photography with an organic, linen-like comfort. Also acts as text color across dark emerald sections.
- **Neutral Container (`#E6F0E9` — Mint):** Soft contextual tint used for alternate carousel panels, inset product spec sheets, and secondary card containers.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional dialogue between classical French pastry menus and high-clarity transactional design:
- **Playfair Display (Headings, Display, & Prices):** Carries the lyrical, high-contrast personality of historical gastronomy. Used with tight negative tracking on major headlines to command visual gravitas. It brings artisanal character to menu items, collection titles, and standalone prices.
- **Inter (Body, Meta, & Labels):** Acts as the neutral, utilitarian counterweight. Crisp apertures ensure legible ingredient lists, allergen notes, and checkout interactions across all screen densities.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase with wide tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mimic embossed gold-foil packaging seals and boutique tier stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a fluid 12-column grid system bounded by generous exterior margins to preserve the feel of an upscale art publication:
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, max content container width of 1320px, 48px outer canvas margins, and 24px column gutters. Section transitions deploy generous vertical spacing (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to let hero pastries breathe.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, 32px canvas margins, and 20px gutters. Content cards reflow from 3 or 4 columns down to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column grid, 20px margins, and 16px gutters. Product listings stack vertically or scroll along horizontal edge-to-edge carousels with peek pagination.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile and organic rather than mechanical, avoiding stark dropshadows in favor of soft, luminous light passes:
- **Golden Ambient Glows:** Elevated elements (such as featured seasonal tasting boxes and cart drawers) cast diffused, warm ambient shadows: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 61, 52, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(200, 169, 106, 0.12)`.
- **Gilded Hairline Outlines:** Floating panels, dialogs, and cards are outlined with delicate 1px borders using `rgba(200, 169, 106, 0.35)` on dark backgrounds and `rgba(200, 169, 106, 0.45)` on light backgrounds.
- **Tonal Layering:** Visual hierarchy relies on surface nesting—Cream base backgrounds stack Mint cards, which contain Emerald Deep callouts and burnished gold badges.

## Shapes

The form language echoes smooth pastry glazes, rounded ganache cuts, and handcrafted confection molds:
- **Cards & Surfaces (`rounded-2xl` / 1.5rem / 24px):** Product cards, interactive modals, and imagery containers feature soft, sweeping corners.
- **Buttons & Chips (Pill-shaped / 9999px):** Action elements use full-pill contours, providing an inviting, tactile contrast against rectangular content blocks.
- **Image Cutouts:** Signature baked goods leverage gentle arching crowns or soft rounded corners to evoke custom gift packaging and bakery cloches.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background in Emerald Deep (`#0F3D34`), text in Cream (`#F7F6F1`) with Inter semi-bold typography, full pill radius, and padding `14px 28px`. Subtle hairline border in Gold (`rgba(200, 169, 106, 0.4)`). Hover state shifts surface to Emerald (`#134E43`) with an expanded warm gold shadow.
- **Secondary / Gilded Button:** Transparent background, 1.5px border in Gold (`#C8A96A`), text in Emerald Deep (`#0F3D34`). Hover fills background with Mint (`#E6F0E9`).
- **Dark Section Button:** Gold (`#C8A96A`) solid background with Emerald Deep text for prominent conversion points on dark hero or footer areas.

### Cards
- **Product & Tasting Cards:** Surface in Mint (`#E6F0E9`) or Pure White over Cream, corner radius `rounded-2xl` (24px), enclosed by a 1px border of `rgba(200, 169, 106, 0.35)`. Contains generous internal padding (`space-lg`), high-resolution product photography, item titles in Playfair Display, and price tags highlighted in Gold.
- **Hero & Curated Feature Cards:** Dark Emerald Deep (`#0F3D34`) canvas with Cream text, gold leaf divider rules, and full-bleed macro photography.

### Chips & Badges
- **Artisanal Badges:** Used for 'Single Origin', 'Batch Reserve', or 'Gluten-Friendly'. Rendered as compact pill containers with a Mint background, 1px Gold border, and uppercase tracked text in Emerald Deep.
- **Dietary & Tasting Filters:** Outlined pills with 1px border (`#C8A96A`). Selected state fills with Emerald Deep and switches label to Cream.

### Lists & Menus
- **Patisserie Ledger:** Traditional French menu style. Left-aligned pastry name in Playfair Display and flavor notes in Inter, followed by an elegant dotted gold baseline leader connecting directly to right-aligned gold pricing.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Controls:** Custom circular geometry with a 1.5px Gold outline. Active state features a smooth inner dot in Emerald Deep against a Mint bed.

### Input Fields
- **Bespoke Fields:** Background in pure Cream or white, 1px border in `rgba(15, 61, 52, 0.2)`. Focus transitions the border to solid Gold (`#C8A96A`) paired with a subtle `0 0 0 3px rgba(200, 169, 106, 0.2)` glow. Labels rest above in uppercase Inter (`label-md`).

### Additional Signature Components
- **Flourish Dividers:** Hairline rules featuring a centered miniature diamond or botanical pastry emblem in Gold.
- **Bespoke Ribbon Seal:** A floating circular seal (`#C8A96A` background, `#0F3D34` text) reading 'Baked Slow' positioned over top-tier gifting sets.
